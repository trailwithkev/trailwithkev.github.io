Critical Reminders
Before You Go
Book these now — some fill within minutes of opening.
🪜
Manitou Incline Reservation
Free but mandatory. Book at coloradosprings.gov/incline. Opens Jul 1 at 9 AM MT sharp for July slots. Afternoon slot for Tue Jul 14 — set a reminder now.
🚌
RFTA Maroon Bells Shuttle
Required for Maroon Bells access. Book at rfta.com ~2 weeks before Aug 1. Also reserve Aspen Highlands parking at aspenparking.com.
⚡
14er Lightning Rule
All 14er starts depart TH at 3:45 AM. Summit before 10 AM every time. Turn around at any lightning — no exceptions, not 200 ft from the top.
⛑️
Mt. Sneffels Helmet
Class 3 summit ridge scramble on Sneffels requires a climbing helmet. Pack it before leaving Framingham. Not optional on this route.
🌡️
Altitude — First Week
Coming from sea level. Drink 4–5L water daily. No alcohol for the first 3 days. Mild headaches are normal. The gentle Week 1 schedule is intentional.
💻
Confirm Wifi at All Airbnbs
WFH is non-negotiable. Message every host before booking: "Does your property have reliable high-speed wifi?" Get it in writing.
👨‍👦
Dad on 14er Days
Sneffels (Jul 25), Handies (Jul 26), Sherman (Aug 2), Quandary (Aug 8), Democrat (Aug 9) — dad rests at Airbnb on all 14er days. Plan activities for him on those mornings.
🛣️
Kite Lake TH Road
CR-8 last 2 miles is dirt. Drive slowly and carefully in a sedan — perfectly passable but not for rushing. Allow extra time on the drive to the TH.
