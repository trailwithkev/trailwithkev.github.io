Trip at a Glance
Twelve Days of Wild America
Three of America's greatest national parks back to back — scan for wolves and grizzlies at dawn in Yellowstone's Lamar Valley, watch geysers erupt from Grand Prismatic Spring, catch sunrise over the Tetons at Mormon Row, hike to an active glacier in Glacier National Park, and wrap up in Bozeman before the flight home. All camping, all wild.

Route & Stops
The American West Route
Begin in Yellowstone's Lamar Valley for wildlife at dusk, then base at Colter Bay in Grand Teton for four nights of geysers, canyons, and mountain hikes. Drive eight hours north to Glacier NP for four nights of glacier hikes and alpine trails. Finish at a farm stay outside Bozeman before flying home.

🐺 Evening Wildlife Stroll — Lamar Valley
Wildlife
Lamar Valley is the best place in Yellowstone to spot wolves — scan the 12-mile stretch from the Little America area near Specimen Ridge Trailhead all the way to the Lamar River Trail. Key spots:
  • Slough Creek area near Dave's Hill
  • The Little America plains
  • Soda Butte Creek — best for grizzly bears
  • Near Soda Butte — pronghorn antelope
  • Warm Creek Picnic Area — moose
  • Along the Lamar River — bison herds
  • Near the confluence — elk
~1.5 hr drive from camp Bring binoculars Bear spray required

🏞️ Grand Canyon of Yellowstone
Iconic
The canyon's rhyolite walls glow yellow and orange above the roaring Yellowstone River. Hit both rims:
  • South Rim — Artist Point (classic postcard view of the Lower Falls)
  • North Rim — Brink of the Lower Falls Trail (Easy · 0.5 mi · 256 ft · ~30 min)
  • North Rim — Lookout Point (short walk from Brink trailhead)
  • North Rim — Red Rock Point (Easy · 0.6 mi · 246 ft · ~30 min)
  • Inspiration Point — drive-up overlook, sweeping canyon view
Artist Point — South Rim Brink of Lower Falls — 0.5 mi Red Rock Point — 0.6 mi Inspiration Point — drive-up
Afternoon

🌋 Old Faithful
Iconic
Time your visit using the latest eruption predictions posted at the visitor center. The geyser erupts roughly every 90 minutes and shoots water 100–180 ft in the air for about 1.5–5 minutes.
Check eruption prediction board ~90 min interval Visitor center nearby
Afternoon
🎨 Grand Prismatic Spring
Iconic
The largest hot spring in the US — a vivid rainbow of orange, yellow, and deep blue from the mineral-rich waters. Park at the Grand Prismatic Spring Parking Lot and walk the boardwalk for ground-level views, or hike up the Fairy Falls trailhead for the classic aerial perspective.
Grand Prismatic Parking Lot Boardwalk loop Fairy Falls overlook optional

🦬 Hayden Valley Wildlife
Wildlife
One of the best spots in Yellowstone to see bison, grizzly bears, wolves, and elk. Drive slowly through the valley and pull over at any of the roadside turnouts — the evening hours are prime wildlife time.

🧊 Grinnell Glacier Trail
Hike
One of the most spectacular hikes in North America — through wildflower meadows, past turquoise lakes, and up to an active glacier at the base of the Continental Divide. The boat ride shortcut is available but the full trail is the real experience (no cheating).
  • Boat ride option available from Many Glacier Hotel dock
  • Trail climbs past Swiftcurrent and Josephine Lakes
  • Final push up to the glacial moraine
  • Grinnell Lake glows bright teal at the base
Hard · 10.9 mi · 2,080 ft gain · ~5h 18m Bear spray required Many Glacier trailhead
Afternoon
🏔️ Hidden Lake Overlook
Hike
A short but stunning hike from Logan Pass to an overlook above deep-blue Hidden Lake, framed by the Garden Wall. Bighorn sheep are commonly spotted on the rocky slopes.
Easy · 2.7 mi · 551 ft gain · ~1h 21m Logan Pass trailhead Bighorn sheep common here

🥾 Haystack Butte via Highline Trail
Hike
Glacier's most famous trail — a narrow ledge path carved into the cliff face above Logan Pass with dramatic views across the entire park. The route to Haystack Butte offers sweeping vistas of the Livingston Range and the Going-to-the-Sun corridor.
Hard · 8.4 mi · 1,758 ft gain Logan Pass trailhead Narrow cliff ledge section at start Bear spray required

Hike
