Trip at a Glance
Eleven Days in the Evergreen State
Three of Washington's national parks plus a Bavarian mountain town and a volcano in between — a late-night drive to the Olympic Peninsula for the Mt Storm King scramble above Lake Crescent and mountain goats on Hurricane Ridge, wildflower meadows and a fire lookout on Mount Rainier's Skyline and Sunrise trails, orchard-town hiking and Colchuck Lake near Leavenworth, and larch-lined alpine lakes plus Mount Baker in the North Cascades before closing out in Seattle.

Route & Stops
The Pacific Northwest Loop
Fly into Seattle late on Day 1 and drive straight to the Olympic Peninsula for two nights near Port Angeles. Head south and east to Mount Rainier for two nights of wildflower meadows and alpine views, then over to Leavenworth for two nights in the Bavarian foothills. Finish with three nights in the North Cascades chasing larches, alpine lakes, and a day trip out to Mount Baker, then drive back to Seattle for a night out before exploring the city and flying home.

🌄 Sourdough Ridge Trail to Mount Fremont Lookout
Moderate Hike
Starts on Sourdough Ridge Trail from the Sunrise Visitor Center before joining up to Mount Fremont Lookout — a historic fire lookout tower with sweeping views of Rainier, the Sunrise valley, and out toward Mount Baker — timed for sunset. At this pace, summit is around 7:15–7:25 PM, right at sunset (7:38 PM) — the hike back down to the trailhead will be after dark, so bring headlamps. Pack snacks to eat up at the lookout — dinner's back at camp.
5.7 mi round trip · Moderate 🔦 Headlamps required for the hike back Sourdough Ridge Trailhead Parking 🔗 AllTrails
