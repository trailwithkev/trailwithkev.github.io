Trip at a Glance
Eleven Days in the Evergreen State
Three of Washington's national parks plus a Bavarian mountain town in between — a late-night drive to the Olympic Peninsula for the Mt Storm King scramble above Lake Crescent and mountain goats on Hurricane Ridge, wildflower meadows and glacier views on Mount Rainier's Skyline and Burroughs trails, orchard-town hiking and beer gardens in Leavenworth, and larch-lined alpine lakes in the North Cascades before closing out in Seattle.

Route & Stops
The Pacific Northwest Loop
Fly into Seattle late on Day 1 and drive straight to the Olympic Peninsula for two nights near Port Angeles. Head south and east to Mount Rainier for two nights of wildflower meadows and alpine views, then over to Leavenworth for two nights in the Bavarian foothills. Finish with three nights in the North Cascades chasing larches and alpine lakes before looping back to Seattle to fly home.

Arrive Seattle Late Night · Drive to Port Angeles · Hike Mt Storm King
✈️ Late-night landing at SEA ⛺ Camp at Salish Trails RV & Campground 🥾 Mt Storm King
Fri, Sept 4 — Day 1 of 11
›
🚗  Salish Trails RV & Campground (80 O'Brien Rd, Port Angeles, WA 98362) is ~3 hrs from SEA via US-101 around Hood Canal — with a late flight, plan on rolling in well after midnight.
Late Night
✈️ Land at Seattle–Tacoma (SEA) · Pick Up Rental Car
Arrival
A late flight in — grab the rental car and start straight out toward the Olympic Peninsula rather than waiting for morning.
Fuel up near the airport
Overnight
🚗 Drive to Port Angeles — Salish Trails RV & Campground
Drive
~3 hr overnight drive west via US-101, skirting Hood Canal — quiet roads this late, but watch for deer.
80 O'Brien Rd, Port Angeles, WA 98362
Early Morning
⛺ Set Up Camp — Salish Trails RV & Campground
Camp
Home base for both Olympic nights, just outside Port Angeles. Pitch the tent as the sun comes up and grab a few hours before the day gets going.
Morning
🛒 Groceries — Walmart, Port Angeles
Errand
Stock up on food, water, and camp supplies in town before heading out — options thin out fast west of Port Angeles.
Afternoon
🥾 Hike Mt Storm King
Iconic Hike
A steep, exposed scramble above Lake Crescent, with fixed ropes on the final push — one of the best view-to-effort payoffs on the Peninsula. Take it slow after a night with no sleep.
~4 mi round trip · ~2,000 ft gain Rope section near the summit
2
Sol Duc Falls · Hurricane Ridge · Mountain Goats
💦 Sol Duc Falls 🐐 Hurricane Ridge mountain goats
Sat, Sept 5 — Day 2 of 11
›
🚗  Sol Duc Falls trailhead is ~40 min from Salish Trails; Hurricane Ridge is ~45 min in the opposite direction — do Sol Duc first thing, then loop back through Port Angeles for Hurricane Ridge. Keep distance from goats, they're drawn to salt and can be aggressive.
Morning
💦 Sol Duc Falls Trail
Hike
A short, easy hike through old-growth forest to a striking three-way waterfall split by basalt columns.
1.6 mi round trip
Afternoon
🐐 Hurricane Ridge — Alpine Meadows & Mountain Goats
Wildlife
Sweeping views back into the interior Olympic Mountains — and the best odds on this trip of spotting mountain goats grazing the ridgeline meadows. Stay well back and never approach; give them the trail.
Hurricane Hill Trail — 3.2 mi Binoculars recommended
Evening
🍽️ Dinner at Salish Trails
Dinner
Back to camp for dinner — last night on the Peninsula before the drive to Rainier tomorrow.
3
Lake Crescent Morning · Drive to Mount Rainier · Cougar Rock Campground
🌄 Lake Crescent & Marymere Falls 🚗 Drive to Rainier ⛺ Camp at Cougar Rock
Sun, Sept 6 — Day 3 of 11
›
🚗  Cougar Rock Campground is ~3.5 hrs from Port Angeles via US-101 and WA-7/706 — grab any last groceries in Ashford before entering the park, no cell service inside.
Morning
🌄 Lake Crescent & Marymere Falls — Quick Stop
Easy Walk
A last easy walk on the Peninsula before the long drive south — Marymere Falls is a short forested loop right off the highway, with Lake Crescent's deep blue water as a backdrop.
1.8 mi round trip
Midday
🚗 Break Camp · Drive to Mount Rainier
Drive
Long drive south and east around Puget Sound to the Nisqually entrance of Mount Rainier National Park. Stock up on groceries and fuel in Ashford before the park entrance — options thin out fast once inside.
~3.5 hrs
Evening
⛺ Arrive & Set Up Camp — Cougar Rock Campground
Camp
Cougar Rock sits inside the park near the Nisqually entrance, a short drive from Paradise. Set up camp and get oriented before the light fades — Christine Falls and Narada Falls are quick roadside stops on the way in if there's time.
4
Skyline Trail · Paradise · Wildflower Meadows
🥾 Skyline Trail Loop 💐 Wildflower meadows at Paradise
Mon, Sept 7 — Day 4 of 11
›
🚗  Paradise is ~20 min from Cougar Rock — arrive early, the lot fills up by mid-morning even in September.
Morning
🥾 Skyline Trail Loop — Paradise
Hike
The signature Rainier day hike — a 5.5-mile loop climbing through late-season wildflower meadows to Panorama Point, with the mountain looming directly overhead the entire way.
5.5 mi · ~1,700 ft gain Best views of Rainier's summit
Afternoon
💦 Myrtle Falls & Paradise Visitor Center
Scenic
A short walk from the Skyline trailhead to Myrtle Falls, framed perfectly against the mountain. Pop into the visitor center for trail conditions and ranger talks.
0.8 mi round trip
Evening
🔥 Campfire & Stargazing
Camp
Dinner at camp, then a campfire if there's a burn ban exception — Rainier's dark skies are worth staying up for on a clear night.
5
Sunrise & Burroughs Mountain Trail · Drive to Leavenworth
🌅 Sunrise (White River side) 🥾 Burroughs Mountain Trail 🚗 Drive to Leavenworth
Tue, Sept 8 — Day 5 of 11
›
🚗  Sunrise is ~1.5 hrs from Cougar Rock around the east side of the mountain — leave camp before dawn. From Sunrise it's another ~2.5–3 hrs to Leavenworth via Chinook Pass (SR-410) and US-2 — confirm the pass is still open before you go, it can close with early snow.
Pre-Dawn
🚗 Drive to Sunrise — Highest Point Reachable by Car
Drive
Sunrise Point catches the first light hitting Rainier's summit — worth the early alarm and the long way around.
~1.5 hr drive
Morning
🏔️ Burroughs Mountain Trail
Iconic Hike
One of the best views in the Cascades — an alpine tundra trail leading to the closest you can get to Rainier's glaciers without a permit. Push to Second Burroughs for the full payoff if legs and weather allow.
9 mi round trip to Second Burroughs ~2,900 ft gain Check for snow/ice in early Sept
Afternoon
🚗 Break Camp · Drive to Leavenworth via Chinook Pass
Drive
East over Chinook Pass and down US-2 into the dry side of the Cascades — a striking change in scenery from alpine glaciers to pine and orchard country.
~2.5–3 hrs
Evening
⛺ Set Up Camp Near Leavenworth · Stroll the Bavarian Village
Camp
Set up camp just outside town, then walk the Bavarian-themed village for dinner — a strange, welcome contrast after five days in the backcountry.
6
Icicle Gorge Loop · Leavenworth Bavarian Village
🥾 Icicle Gorge Loop 🍺 Bavarian Village
Wed, Sept 9 — Day 6 of 11
›
🚗  Icicle Creek Road trailheads are 15–20 min from town — fill up on gas before heading up the canyon, and grab a Northwest Forest Pass if you don't already have one. Colchuck Lake is a tougher, longer option nearby for stronger hikers wanting more elevation.
Morning
🥾 Icicle Gorge Loop
Hike
An easy loop along Icicle Creek through pine forest and granite gorge — a good leg-stretcher after five straight days of bigger mountain hikes.
3.4 mi loop · easy grade
Afternoon
🍺 Bavarian Village — Leavenworth Town
Scenic
A Bavarian-themed town tucked into the Cascade foothills — half-timbered storefronts, German bakeries, and beer gardens along the main strip. Good spot to slow down for an afternoon.
Front Street shops & bakeries
Evening
🍽️ Dinner in Leavenworth
Dinner
German food and a local beer in town before heading back to camp for the night.
7
Leavenworth Morning · Drive to North Cascades · Diablo Lake
☕ Leavenworth bakery run 🚗 Drive to Colonial Creek 💎 Diablo Lake Overlook
Thu, Sept 10 — Day 7 of 11
›
🚗  Leavenworth to Colonial Creek Campground is ~3 hrs via US-2 and WA-20 — Stevens Pass is usually clear this early in September. Fuel up and grab groceries before Marblemount, the last real stop before the park.
Morning
☕ Bakery Run & Break Camp — Leavenworth
Errand
One last coffee and pastry in town before packing up and heading north into the North Cascades.
Midday
🚗 Drive to North Cascades — Colonial Creek Campground
Drive
West on US-2 then north on WA-20 (the North Cascades Highway) into the park. Stock up in Marblemount — services are sparse past it.
~3 hrs Last groceries in Marblemount
Afternoon
⛺ Arrive & Set Up Camp — Colonial Creek Campground
Camp
Right on the shore of Diablo Lake, dead center in the park — a great base for the next few days.
Evening
💎 Diablo Lake Overlook — Sunset
Iconic
The lake's glacial-blue color comes from suspended rock flour — most striking in late light. A quick pullout stop on WA-20.
Roadside pullout
8
Maple Pass Loop · Washington Pass Overlook
🥾 Maple Pass Loop 🍂 Washington Pass Overlook
Fri, Sept 11 — Day 8 of 11
›
🚗  Rainy Pass trailhead is ~45 min east of Colonial Creek on WA-20 — arrive by mid-morning, the lot is small and popular.
Morning
🥾 Maple Pass Loop
Iconic Hike
Widely regarded as one of the best day hikes in Washington — a full loop past Lake Ann, over an alpine ridge, with panoramic views the whole way. Early September can catch the first larches turning gold at higher elevation.
7.2 mi loop · ~2,000 ft gain Lake Ann viewpoint
Afternoon
🍂 Washington Pass Overlook
Scenic
A short paved path to a viewpoint over the jagged Liberty Bell group — one of the most dramatic roadside views on WA-20.
0.3 mi paved
Evening
🍽️ Dinner at Colonial Creek
Dinner
Back to camp for dinner — legs will be tired after Maple Pass.
9
Cascade Pass Trail · Ross Lake Overlook
🥾 Cascade Pass Trail 🏞️ Ross Lake Overlook
Sat, Sept 12 — Day 9 of 11
›
🚗  Cascade Pass trailhead is ~50 min up the gravel Cascade River Road from Marblemount — high-clearance recommended but not required.
Morning
🥾 Cascade Pass Trail
Hike
Switchbacks up through meadows to a high alpine pass surrounded by glaciated peaks — one of the classic North Cascades approaches, with the option to push further to Sahale Arm for those with energy left.
7.4 mi round trip · ~1,800 ft gain Extend to Sahale Arm for more
Afternoon
🏞️ Ross Lake Overlook
Scenic
A quick roadside pullout on WA-20 looking down over Ross Lake's turquoise water, worth the two-minute stop on the way back to camp.
Evening
🔥 Last Night — Campfire at Colonial Creek
Camp
Final night in the mountains — a campfire on the shore of Diablo Lake to close things out before heading to Seattle.
