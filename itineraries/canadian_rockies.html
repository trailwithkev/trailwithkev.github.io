The Rocky Mountain Route
Start in Banff for three nights — your base for Lake Louise, Moraine Lake, and Sulphur Mountain. Then drive north on the Icefields Parkway to the Columbia Icefield, settle into Golden for two nights as base for Yoho and Glacier NP, then loop through Kootenay NP on the way east to Calgary for the flight home.

🥾 Iceline + Little Yoho Valley + Yoho Valley Trail Loop
Hard

Top-10 day hike in all of Canada. Hike clockwise — gets the brutal elevation gain done early and protects the knees on descent. 360° glacier panoramas from the exposed Iceline ridge timed to morning golden hour.

  • 0 km · Takakkaw Falls (1,510 m) — start just past the iconic 373 m waterfall. Sunrise hits the falls at ~5:30 AM if you're near it
  • 0–4 km · Iceline Switchbacks — the hardest climbing of the day, ~500 m gain in steep rocky zigzags. Headlamps until ~5:30 AM, then first light on the valley below
  • ~7 km · Iceline Summit (2,229 m) — the payoff. Six glaciers visible at once: Emerald, Daly, Fairy, Diableret, Niles and Yoho. The Presidents and Vice President peaks dominate the horizon. Alpine tundra ridge walk for several km
  • ~11 km · Little Yoho Valley + Stanley Mitchell Hut — descend into the hanging valley. Alpine Club of Canada hut in a pristine meadow. Eat lunch here beside the Little Yoho River
  • ~15 km · Twin Falls junction — optional 1 km detour to Twin Falls, two side-by-side cascades dropping 90 m
  • ~18 km · Laughing Falls — beautiful 25 m cascade where the Laughing Creek joins the Yoho River
  • ~20.3 km · Back at Takakkaw Falls — finish at the trailhead, ~noon if 4 AM start
20.3 km loop · Hard 1,090 m elevation gain ~7.5–8.5 hrs Clockwise recommended 6 glaciers from the summit Top 10 hike in Canada Layers for the exposed Iceline ridge Parks Canada pass required 🔗 AllTrails

🚂 Lower Spiral Tunnels Viewpoint
Historic
  • The Spiral Tunnels are a feat of 1909 railway engineering — two figure-8 tunnels bored inside Mount Ogden and Cathedral Mountain to reduce the deadly 4.5% "Big Hill" gradient to a safe 2.2%
  • From this viewpoint you can watch a full freight train exit the lower tunnel while its tail is still entering — the train loops 180° inside the mountain, so the head and tail are visible simultaneously
  • ~25–30 freight trains pass daily; no fixed schedule but the wait is usually under 30 min. Check field.ca for Rocky Mountaineer schedule if you want the scenic train
7.4 km east of Field on Hwy 1 ~25–30 trains/day Wait up to 30 min for a train 1909 engineering landmark Head + tail visible at once

Before You Go
Critical Tips
A few things to lock in before you leave — some require advance booking or can't be fixed once you're on the road.
✈️
Flight Reminder
June 20 · 7 AM YYC. Be at the airport by 5:00 AM. Book a hotel within 5 min of the terminal for June 19 night — Hampton Inn or Acclaim Hotel YYC are both solid.
🎫
Book Early
Parks Canada shuttle (Lake Louise / Moraine Lake), Banff Gondola, and Columbia Icefield Glacier Adventure + Skywalk all sell out in June. Also pick up a Parks Canada Discovery Pass — covers all national parks on this trip.
🐻
Wildlife Safety
Bear spray on every trail. Make noise while hiking. June is peak bear activity season in all four national parks. Keep food in the car, not in camp.
🌡️
June Weather
Expect 10–18°C, afternoon showers possible. Layer up and pack a waterproof shell. Snow is still possible near the Icefield — dress like it's October near the glacier.
