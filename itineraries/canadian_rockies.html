Trip at a Glance
Six Days of Wild
Ride the Parks Canada shuttle to the impossible turquoise of Lake Louise and Moraine Lake, ride the Banff Gondola to the Sulphur Mountain summit, walk on 10,000-year-old glacial ice at the Columbia Icefield, loop Emerald Lake in Yoho, cross 8 bridges over Marble Canyon's limestone gorge, and soak in Radium Hot Springs before the flight home.

Route & Stops
The Rocky Mountain Route
Start in Banff for three nights — your base for Lake Louise, Moraine Lake, and Sulphur Mountain. Then drive north on the Icefields Parkway to the Columbia Icefield, settle into Golden for two nights as base for Yoho and Glacier NP, then loop through Kootenay NP on the way east to Calgary for the flight home.

Before You Go
Critical Tips
A few things to lock in before you leave — some require advance booking or can't be fixed once you're on the road.
✈️
Flight Reminder
June 20 · 7 AM YYC. Be at the airport by 5:00 AM. Book a hotel within 5 min of the terminal for June 19 night — Hampton Inn or Acclaim Hotel YYC are both solid.
🎫
Book Early
Parks Canada shuttle (Lake Louise / Moraine Lake), Banff Gondola, and Columbia Icefield Glacier Adventure + Skywalk all sell out in June. Also pick up a Parks Canada Discovery Pass — covers all national parks on this trip.
🐻
Wildlife Safety
Bear spray on every trail. Make noise while hiking. June is peak bear activity season in all four national parks. Keep food in the car, not in camp.
🌡️
June Weather
Expect 10–18°C, afternoon showers possible. Layer up and pack a waterproof shell. Snow is still possible near the Icefield — dress like it's October near the glacier.
